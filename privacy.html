Privacy Policy

TypeRush: Typing Race · Petalform LLC · Effective September 29, 2026

This policy explains what information the TypeRush mobile app ("TypeRush", "the app") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. TypeRush is operated by Petalform LLC ("we", "us").

Summary

  • We collect only what is needed to run races, keep your progress, prevent cheating and keep the app working.
  • TypeRush only reads text you type inside the in-app race field. It does not install a keyboard, request "Full Access" keyboard permissions, or read anything you type in other apps.
  • We do not sell your personal information and do not show third-party advertising.
  • You can delete your account and personal data at any time in Settings → Account → Delete account.

Typing data

While you race, the app compares what you type against the race passage on your device to move your car. When a race finishes, the app sends the result (time, keystroke and error counts, and a coarse progress timeline) to our servers. We do not store free-form text you type, and we never collect keyboard input outside the race field.

Service providers

We use the following providers to operate the app. They process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — database, authentication and realtime race connections.
  • Apple and Google — optional sign-in, in-app purchases and push notification delivery.
  • Expo — push notification delivery and app updates.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management.
  • Sentry — crash reporting.
  • PostHog — anonymous product analytics (can be disabled in Settings).

What other players can see

Your username, display name, avatar, level, rank, equipped vehicle, selected title, achievements and race statistics are visible to other players (for example on leaderboards, in races and on your profile). Your email address is never shown.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, progress, vehicles, friends, challenges and notification tokens. Race records that involved other players are kept only in anonymized form (no link to you) so other players' results remain accurate. Guest accounts that are signed out cannot be recovered.

Children

TypeRush is intended for players aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, or to object to certain processing. You can delete your account in the app, or contact us for other requests.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections, and access to game data is restricted by server-side security rules. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here and update the effective date.
